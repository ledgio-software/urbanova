1\. About This Document

This is the master plan for building URBANOVA from an idea into a registered business, a live online store, and a working youth movement with its own support fund. It brings together the earlier documents into one order of work, so the team always knows what to do next.

1.1 How the Plan Works

- The work is split into seven phases, numbered 0 to 6.

- Each phase has a goal, steps, owners, deliverables and a budget list.

- Each phase ends with a phase gate: a short checklist that must be complete before moving to the next phase. Gates stop the team from launching before the basics are safe and legal.

- Some phases overlap. For example, website building (Phase 2) can start while registration (Phase 1) is being processed.

1.2 Guiding Principles

- Store first, then movement. The brand earns the money that funds the movement, so a working store comes before big programs.

- Start small, prove it, then grow. Launch with one or two programs done well, not five done badly.

- Legal and safe before public. No under-18 joins and no donation is taken before the relevant policies and accounts exist.

- Be honest in public. Publish what was raised and what it achieved.

*Timings are estimates for a small founding team working part-time. Adjust them to your real capacity. Owners are suggested roles (Section 10) until the team is confirmed.*

4\. Phase 1: Legal Setup & Registration

|                |                                                                                                      |
|----------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Goal**       | Register the brand business and the Movement, open separate accounts, and meet data protection rules |
| **Timing**     | Months 1–3 (government processing times vary)                                                        |
| **Lead**       | Ops lead + Finance lead                                                                              |
| **Depends on** | Phase 0 decisions and founders' agreement                                                            |

Why This Phase Matters

Registration lets URBANOVA open business accounts, use a payment gateway, sign partner agreements and apply for grants. Keeping the brand and the Movement as separate entities with separate accounts keeps sales money and donations apart, which donors, grant-givers and tax authorities expect.

Steps

| **\#** | **Task**                                                                                        | **Owner**    | **Reference**       |
|--------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|--------------|---------------------|
| 1      | Get advice from a lawyer or accountant on the right structure for each arm                      | Ops lead     | Movement doc, s.3   |
| 2      | Get Tax Identification Numbers (TINs) for all founders, directors and the secretary             | Ops lead     | Ghana Card needed   |
| 3      | Register the brand business with the Registrar General's Department                             | Ops lead     | Movement doc, s.11  |
| 4      | Register the Movement as a company limited by guarantee (2+ directors, a secretary, an auditor) | Ops lead     | Movement doc, s.11  |
| 5      | Appoint an auditor and get their consent letter                                                 | Finance lead |                     |
| 6      | Open separate bank and mobile money accounts: brand, Movement                                   | Finance lead | Fraud policy, s.18  |
| 7      | Ask about protecting the URBANOVA name and logo as a trademark                                  | Ops lead     | Confirm with lawyer |
| 8      | Register with the Data Protection Commission                                                    | Ops lead     | Policies doc, s.3   |
| 9      | Sign up with a payment provider (e.g. Paystack) using the business registration                 | Finance lead | Build Instructions  |
| 10     | Fill in all placeholders in the Policies document and get a lawyer to review it                 | Ops lead     | Policies doc        |
| 11     | Set up simple bookkeeping (spreadsheet or accounting app) from day one                          | Finance lead |                     |

Deliverables

- Business registration certificate and TIN

- Movement registration certificate

- Separate bank and mobile money accounts

- Data Protection Commission registration number

- Payment provider account (test mode)

- Lawyer-reviewed Policies and Terms

Phase Gate: Ready to Move On When…

> ☐ Both entities registered (or the brand registered and the Movement filing submitted)
>
> ☐ Separate accounts open; no business money in personal accounts
>
> ☐ Data Protection Commission registration done
>
> ☐ Payment provider account approved
>
> ☐ Policies reviewed by a lawyer and placeholders filled in

Budget Items

- Registration fees (brand and Movement)

- Lawyer and accountant fees

- Auditor fee (yearly)

11.1 Budget Rules

- Keep a reserve of at least \[10\]% of each phase's budget for surprises.

12\. Risks & How to Handle Them

| **Risk**                            | **What could happen**                    | **How to reduce it**                                           |
|-------------------------------------|------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Running out of cash**             | Production or launch stalls              | Small first runs; pre-orders; strict budget; reserve           |
| **Registration delays**             | Cannot take payments or donations        | Start early; build the website in parallel                     |
| **Poor product quality**            | Returns, bad reviews, damaged reputation | Approve samples; check every batch                             |
| **Name or logo copied**             | Fake URBANOVA products                   | Secure domain and handles; seek trademark protection           |
| **Delivery problems**               | Late or lost orders, angry customers     | Reliable partner; clear tracking; resend or refund fast        |
| **Fraud**                           | Fake payments, chargebacks               | Dispatch only after verified payment; check odd orders         |
| **Harm to a young member**          | Serious harm and loss of trust           | Safeguarding policy, screened mentors, no unsupervised contact |
| **Mixing brand and Movement money** | Loss of donor trust, tax problems        | Separate accounts; two-person approval; annual audit           |
| **Doing too much at once**          | Programs run badly, team burns out       | Follow the phases; launch only one or two programs first       |
| **Founder disagreement**            | Decisions stall, partnership breaks      | Founders' agreement; clear roles and decision process          |
