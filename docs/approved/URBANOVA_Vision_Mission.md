1\. About This Document

This is the founding statement of URBANOVA. It explains who we are, why we exist, what we are working toward, and the values that guide every decision we make, whether that is designing a T-shirt, choosing a partner, or deciding who receives support from our fund.

It is written for everyone who touches URBANOVA:

- **The founding team,** as the reference point when decisions get hard.

- **Young people joining the movement,** so they know what they are part of.

- **Partners, sponsors and funders,** so they understand what they are backing.

- **Customers,** so they know that what they wear stands for something.

- **Registration and grant applications,** where a clear vision and mission are usually required.

*The wording is a draft for the founders to edit. The statements in Section 12 are written so they can be copied directly into registration forms, the website and pitch materials.*

2\. Who We Are

URBANOVA is a streetwear brand and a youth movement, built around one idea: a city of the bold.

The name joins two words. “Urban” is the city: fast, crowded, competitive, full of energy and opportunity. “Nova” is a star that suddenly bursts into brightness. Together they describe what happens when a young person decides to stop waiting and start shining.

URBANOVA is not a place on a map. It is a state of mind that every ambitious young person already carries inside them. The clothing gives that mindset something to wear. The movement gives it somewhere to belong.

2.1 What URBANOVA Is Made Of

| **Part**         | **What it is**                                                                   | **What it does**                                                                                             |
|------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **The Brand**    | A streetwear label selling URBANOVA-branded T-shirts, hoodies and other clothing | Turns the bold-city identity into something people can wear, and earns the money that keeps everything going |
| **The Movement** | A community of young people, mentors and partner businesses                      | Helps young people grow their own ideas and ventures, and gives them a platform to be seen                   |
| **The Fund**     | Money set aside from sales, partners and supporters                              | Gives practical help to young people who need it most                                                        |

Every URBANOVA purchase supports the movement. Every member of the movement carries the brand forward. Each part makes the others stronger.

3\. Why We Exist

3.1 The Problem We See

Young people are full of ideas, talent and drive. Many are already designing, cooking, filming, coding, styling, selling and building. But too many of them run into the same walls:

- **Pressure to fit in.** Young people are often told to tone themselves down, follow a set path, and wait their turn. Many hide what makes them different.

- **No one sees their work.** A great product or service goes nowhere if nobody knows it exists. Advertising costs money that young founders do not have.

- **No one to ask.** Most young people starting out have no mentor, no network, and no one who has done it before.

- **No starting capital.** Even a small amount for tools, materials or training is out of reach for many, and borrowing is risky.

- **Going it alone.** Building something is lonely without a community that understands the hustle.

3.2 Our Answer

URBANOVA exists to break down those walls. We give young people:

| **The wall**               | **What URBANOVA offers**                                                                      |
|----------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Pressure to fit in**     | An identity and community that celebrates being bold and being yourself                       |
| **No one sees their work** | A platform: our website, social media, events and newsletter, used to showcase their ventures |
| **No one to ask**          | Mentors from our partner businesses and community                                             |
| **No starting capital**    | A support fund for tools, materials, training and small grants                                |
| **Going it alone**         | A movement of people who are building too                                                     |

3.3 Why Streetwear?

Clothing is one of the first ways young people express who they are. Streetwear in particular grew out of city culture: it was built by young people, for young people, outside the traditional fashion system. That makes it the natural home for a movement about confidence and self-made success. When someone wears URBANOVA, they are making a statement before they say a word.

4\. Our Vision

Our vision describes the future we are working toward: the world we want to see because URBANOVA exists.

**A generation of young people who are proud to be exactly who they are, and who have the community, the platform and the support to build something of their own.**

4.1 What the Vision Means

- **“A generation”:** we think big. This is not about a handful of success stories. It is about changing what is normal for young people.

- **“Proud to be exactly who they are”:** confidence and self-expression come first. No one should have to hide their style, their ideas or their background to succeed.

- **“The community”:** nobody builds alone. Young people deserve people around them who believe in them.

- **“The platform”:** being seen matters. Good work deserves an audience.

- **“The support”:** practical help: mentoring, skills, tools and money when it counts.

- **“To build something of their own”:** a business, a brand, a career, a creative project. Ownership and independence are the goal.

4.2 Picture the Vision Realised

If URBANOVA succeeds, this is what the city of the bold looks like:

- A young designer sells her first collection to customers she met through a URBANOVA Spotlight feature.

- A teenager who was told his ideas were unrealistic now runs a small business, and mentors the next member who joins.

- Local businesses compete to partner with URBANOVA because it is where the most driven young talent gathers.

- Wearing URBANOVA is recognised as a sign that you back yourself and you back the people around you.

- Young people who once had no one to ask now have a whole movement behind them.

4.3 Short Vision (for logos, banners and social bios)

**A city of the bold, built by the young.**

5\. Our Mission

Our mission describes what we do every day to move toward the vision: who we serve, what we offer, and how we do it.

**To give young people the confidence, visibility and practical support to turn their ideas into real ventures, through a bold streetwear brand, a network of partner businesses, and a fund that helps those who need it most.**

5.1 The Mission in Five Parts

| **Mission part**            | **What we do**                                                         | **How it shows up**                                                                |
|-----------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **1. Inspire confidence**   | Create a brand and culture that celebrates boldness and being yourself | Clothing, campaigns, stories and events that make young people feel seen and proud |
| **2. Create visibility**    | Put young founders and creatives in front of real audiences            | Spotlight features, social media, marketplace listings, events, newsletter         |
| **3. Connect people**       | Link young people with mentors, partners and each other                | Mentor matching, partner programs, community meet-ups                              |
| **4. Provide support**      | Give practical help where it makes the biggest difference              | Starter Fund grants, tools and materials, sponsored training                       |
| **5. Sustain the movement** | Earn and raise money responsibly so the work continues                 | Brand sales, a fixed give-back share, partners, supporters and grants              |

5.2 Short Mission (for forms and pitch decks)

**We help bold young people be themselves, get seen, and build what they believe in.**

5.3 What the Mission Is Not

Being clear about what we will not do protects the mission:

- We are not a fashion label that uses a good cause as marketing. The movement is real work, with published results.

- We are not a lender. We do not put young people into debt.

- We do not promise riches or guaranteed success. We offer a fair chance and real support.

- We do not tell young people who to be. We help them become more of who they already are.

6\. Our Core Values

Our values are the rules we hold ourselves to. When a decision is unclear, we come back to these five.

6.1 Boldness

We act with courage. We would rather try, fail and learn than never try at all. We back young people who are willing to put themselves out there.

**How we live it:**

- Our designs, voice and campaigns are confident and unapologetic

- We support members whose ideas are early and unpolished, not just the ones already succeeding

- We take smart risks on new programs, and say so openly when something does not work

*What it is not: being reckless, loud for the sake of it, or putting young people in harm's way.*

6.2 Authenticity

We celebrate people as they are. Every member's story is told in their own words. The URBANOVA identity is aspirational, but never fake.

**How we live it:**

- No dress code, style or pitch format is imposed on members

- Member stories are edited for clarity, never rewritten to sound like someone else

- We never use stock images or invented stories to stand in for real members

*What it is not: everyone looking or sounding the same, or chasing trends that are not true to us.*

6.3 Community

Nobody rises alone. Members, mentors, partners and customers look out for each other, and everyone who receives support is encouraged to give some back.

**How we live it:**

- Members feature and recommend each other

- Members who grow are invited to mentor those starting out

- We build spaces, online and in person, where people meet and work together

*What it is not: a closed club or a popularity contest.*

6.4 Integrity

We do what we say. We are honest about money, fair in our decisions, and careful with the trust young people, families and partners place in us.

**How we live it:**

- Brand and movement money are kept separate, and we publish where funds go

- Support decisions are made by more than one person, with reasons recorded

- We protect members' privacy and safety, especially those under 18

- Partners cannot buy influence over who gets featured or funded

*What it is not: telling people what they want to hear, or cutting corners because no one is watching.*

6.5 Opportunity

Talent is everywhere; opportunity is not. We open doors for young people from every background, and give priority to those with the least access.

**How we live it:**

- Joining the movement is free

- Support goes first to those with fewest resources and connections

- We share knowledge freely: pricing, marketing, photography, customer service

*What it is not: charity that makes people feel small. We offer a hand up, with respect.*

8\. Our Commitments

A mission means little without promises people can hold us to.

8.1 To Young People

- Membership will always be free.

- We will treat your story, image and work with respect, and only share them with your permission.

- You keep full ownership of your business, brand and designs.

- Selection for support will be fair and open, and you will get feedback if you are not chosen.

- We will keep you safe. Anyone under 18 needs a parent or guardian's consent before we publish their details.

8.2 To Our Customers

- Our clothing will be made to a standard we are proud of.

- We will tell you exactly how much of your purchase goes to the movement.

- We will report every year on what your purchases helped achieve.

8.3 To Our Partners

- We will use your support for exactly what we agreed.

- We will report back regularly on members supported and money spent.

- We will only use your name and logo with your approval.

8.4 To the Community

- We will publish an annual report: money in, money out, people supported, and lessons learned.

- We will run URBANOVA honestly and lawfully, with proper accounts and oversight.

10\. How We Will Live It

A vision only matters if it shapes everyday choices. These are tests the team can use when making decisions:

| **Question**                                                                        | **If the answer is no…**               |
|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------|
| **Does this help young people be themselves, get seen, or build something?**        | It is probably not part of the mission |
| **Would we be comfortable if every member and partner saw exactly how we decided?** | Change the process before going ahead  |
| **Does this treat young people with respect and keep them safe?**                   | Stop                                   |
| **Is this honest about what we can and cannot deliver?**                            | Rewrite the promise                    |
| **Does this make the movement stronger over the long term, not just this month?**   | Think again                            |

The founders should review this document once a year, and whenever the movement changes significantly. The vision should rarely change. The mission and goals can be updated as URBANOVA learns what works.
